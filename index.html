About this map

The National Drowning Risk Mapping Dashboard is an initiative of the Injury Prevention & Control Unit, Directorate of Non-Communicable Diseases, Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka.

It brings together drowning-risk locations identified in district health submissions to support geographical exploration, water safety awareness, and prevention planning.

Interpreting the map

High- and lower-risk classifications are those reported by the respective districts. A lower-risk designation is not a guarantee that swimming is safe. Some map coordinates may represent approximate locations.

Some records originate from 2024 or earlier reporting periods. The dashboard reflects available reports and is not a real-time safety alert system.

About public reports

New location suggestions and correction requests should be reviewed by the responsible team before publication. Public submissions do not directly edit the official risk map.

PREVENTION & RESPONSE

Water safety and drowning prevention

Key prevention themes for community awareness and district-level planning.

Recognise hazardous locations

Identify deep or fast-moving water, unsafe bathing points, strong coastal currents, unprotected wells and other local hazards.

Prevent exposure

Prioritise child supervision, water barriers, appropriate warning signs and community-level safety education.

Respond safely

Avoid unsafe rescues. Alert emergency services and use available reach or throw rescue techniques without entering hazardous water.

This page provides general water-safety themes, not site-specific clearance or a substitute for official emergency guidance.

Purpose, institution and data interpretation.

NATIONAL WATER SAFETY INITIATIVE

Supporting geographical risk identification across Sri Lanka

The National Drowning Risk Mapping Dashboard is being developed for the Injury Prevention & Control Unit, Directorate of Non-Communicable Diseases, Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka.

It aims to bring district-reported drowning-prone locations into one searchable and interactive geographical view, supporting prevention planning, local coordination and water safety awareness.

About the Injury Prevention & Control Unit

The Directorate of Non-Communicable Diseases serves as the Ministry of Health’s national focal point for injury prevention. Its programmes emphasise surveillance, risk reduction, evidence-informed interventions and multisectoral collaboration.

DATA NOTES

Sources and interpretation

Planned source: district-level drowning risk-area submissions to the Injury Prevention & Control Unit, including reports prepared in 2024 and earlier periods.

“High-risk” and “lower-risk” reflect the submitted district descriptions; they are not calculated mortality rates. Lower-risk does not mean safe.

All 25 districts are navigable, but an absence of displayed markers does not mean absence of drowning risk.
